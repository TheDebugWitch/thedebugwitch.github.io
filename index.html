The Origin Story

It Started in a
Courtroom.

Before any of this, I was a criminal paralegal in Las Vegas. Case files, legal research, defense proceedings, client communication under serious stakes. I learned early that documentation isn't busywork. It's the difference between something getting resolved and something getting lost forever.

Then I walked into a 911 dispatch center and spent six years triaging real emergencies in my hometown in Arizona, the city where I grew up. Not metaphorical emergencies. Real ones. You don't get to say "let me circle back on that." You assess, you decide, you act, and you document it perfectly because lives depend on the record.

That instinct has followed me through every SaaS platform, every support queue, every bug report since. The tools changed. The stakes changed. The wiring never did.

Now I'm adding engineering to the stack. Because I've spent years on the outside of codebases, finding the cracks. It's time to understand the inside too.
